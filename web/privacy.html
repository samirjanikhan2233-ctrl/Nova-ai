Privacy Policy — NOVA AI

Last updated: [DATE]. Developer: muhammdsamirkabirkhan. Contact: [SUPPORT_EMAIL]

This Privacy Policy explains how NOVA AI ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and authentication identifiers, when you create an account or sign in with Google or email/password (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Content you provide: messages you send to the AI chat and text you submit to AI tools (summarizer, translator, writing assistant, etc.), and the AI's responses, stored so you can view your chat history.
  • Subscription and purchase data: your Premium status, subscription product, renewal date, obtained from Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: app interactions, crash logs, and device information, used to keep the app reliable and secure (via Firebase).
  • Advertising data: if you are on the Free plan, our ad partner (Google AdMob) may collect the advertising ID and related data to show and measure ads, subject to your consent choices.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain AI Chat, chat history, and AI productivity tools.
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To determine and enforce your Free/Premium usage limits.
  • To process and verify subscription purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • To show advertisements to Free plan users (via Google AdMob) and rewarded ads you choose to watch.
  • To improve reliability, detect abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Functions, App Check) — backend infrastructure and data storage.
  • Anthropic — processes the text you submit to generate AI responses. Content is sent to Anthropic's API solely to generate a response and is handled under Anthropic's API data policies.
  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription payments.
  • Google AdMob — serves advertisements to Free plan users.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, as needed to operate the App, or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

Chat history and profile data are retained until you delete a chat, delete your account, or request deletion. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may edit your profile name in Settings.
  • You may delete individual chats or your entire account and data at any time.
  • You may manage ad personalization consent via Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws (access, correction, deletion, portability). Contact us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL] to exercise these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

NOVA AI is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections, Firebase Authentication, and server-side authorization rules, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the App.
